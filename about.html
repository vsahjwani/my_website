About Me

math + data science, UC San Diego

I'm Vani — a math and data science student at UC San Diego, spending most of my time somewhere between a Jupyter notebook and a piano bench.

Most of what I build starts the same way: something looks off, nobody asked me to fix it, and I can't leave it alone. That's how I ended up writing an optimization model for a tutoring program's broken shift schedule, or double-checking five million financial records before trusting what they said. I like the part of the work where a number stops being a guess and starts being something you'd actually bet on.

Right now I'm looking for quant, data science, or applied ML roles — places where the models I build get used to make real decisions, instead of sitting in a slide deck somewhere. What I want out of that is to be learning fast enough that going back for a graduate degree in operations research or statistics feels like the natural next step, not a retreat.

Outside of that, I play piano, take photos, paint, and read more than is strictly useful for a math major — enough that I started a reading club just to have people to argue about books with.
