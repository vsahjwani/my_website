Campus

Founder, Turnt Pages Present

UC San Diego · Apr 2025 – Present

Turnt Pages started from a pretty selfish place: I wanted people to read alongside with, and there wasn't a community for that on campus. The one rule was that everyone reads whatever they want; you just show up to a spot near campus every other Saturday and talk about what you're reading. I expected the lack of a shared text to make discussion harder, however, people talk more freely about a book nobody else has read than one everyone's supposed to have opinions on. This also allowed people to have a dedicated reading time during their week, discuss book suggestions, and make new friends!

Sustainable Investment Group

Equity Researcher → Quantitative Researcher · Oct 2024 – Mar 2026

I joined SIG as an equity researcher, which mostly meant reading through company filings and building the qualitative case for why a name belonged in a sustainability-focused portfolio, and the output was often a well-argued pitch. Moving into the quantitative research seat six months later was less a promotion than a change in what counted as evidence. The qualitative research made me a better quant rather than the other way around as knowing why a company might be mispriced is what tells you which numbers are worth modeling in the first place.

Online Content Writer

Data Science Student Society at UCSD · Part-time · Oct 2024 – Jun 2025

Most of what I wrote for DS3 was aimed at people earlier in the data science pipeline than me: explaining a concept, breaking down a tool, translating something I'd just learned into something a first-year could actually use. It turned out to be a good forcing function as I couldn't write clearly about PCA or a model's assumptions until I could explain why they mattered. Writing for an audience that would immediately notice if I was faking understanding was a great evalutation criteria.
